## 🚀 Selected Work

Full write-ups with screenshots are on [axlee.dev](https://www.axlee.dev).

**At SingHealth**
- **AED-CFR Synthetic Dataset** — simulated 10 years of Singapore cardiac arrests (23,948 patients) that can be shared with Cornell and Purdue. Call counts match the real registry within 10% in all 42 weekly blocks, and no row is a real patient.
- **Penang Ambulance Deployment** — cleaned 45,444 dispatch records in SQL, then co-built a Streamlit dashboard and coverage model for placing Penang's 15 ambulances. The model estimates urgent-call coverage rising from 37.6% to 53.4%.
- **ED Attendance Forecasting** — SARIMAX forecast of 7-day inpatient admissions across 9 hospitals: 2.25% MAPE on the holdout week (down from 3.76%), 5.86% over 609 rolling tests.

**Personal projects**
- **[NTUC Income Eco Run Results Tracker](https://ntuc-income-eco-run-results-tracker.vercel.app/)** — live results for 16,600+ runners across 2024 to 2026, built because the officialsite shows no placement or chip time. Uses a pipelined fetch of up to 50 concurrent requests. My [announcement on r/SingaporeFitness](https://www.reddit.com/r/SingaporeFitness/comments/1sv8n1i/for_those_who_are_curious_about_the_leaderboard/) drew 33K views. ([code](https://github.com/axellee1994/NTUC_Income_Eco_Run_Results_Tracker))
- **[HDB Resale Cluster Forecasting](https://axellee1994.github.io/hdb_resale_cluster_forecasting/)** — K-Means on 243k resale transactions, testing whether unsupervised segmentation improves price forecasting. ([code](https://github.com/axellee1994/hdb_resale_cluster_forecasting))
- **[COE Price Forecasting](https://coe-forecasting.vercel.app/)** — one-round-ahead forecasts for all five COE categories, backtested against a naive baseline. ([code](https://github.com/axellee1994/coe-forecasting))

**Client work**
- **Installment Reminders** — offline Windows app (React, FastAPI, .msi installer) that tells a land seller in Indonesia whom to remind each week and writes the WhatsApp message. 156 automated tests.
- Websites for a Singapore tuition service for students with dyslexia, ADHD and autism, a kids' craft studio in Johor Bahru, and an EHS professional.
